Tax Risk Case Challenge

Meet the auditor, analyse a company’s tax risks, and agree on a strategy to reduce them, using vocabulary from the glossary.

Preparation
15:00

How the session works

  1. 1
    Text retellingRetell the text on the Russian tax system, tax accounting, and filing a tax return to your teacher.
  2. 2
    Case and levelYour teacher tells your pair which case to develop and at which level, A with support or B standard.
  3. 3
    Pair preparationPartner A plays the company. Partner B plays the auditor. Analyse the risks, agree on measures, and choose glossary items.
  4. 4
    Meeting with the auditorAct out the meeting in English. The auditor names the risks, the client responds, and together you agree on an action plan.
  5. 5
    Peer assessmentA student from another pair assesses your role play using the rubric.
  6. 6
    Teacher feedbackThe teacher gives the final mark and one recommendation for improvement.

Presentation requirements

  • Name the main tax risk of the company.
  • Explain at least two reasons for this risk.
  • Propose at least three practical measures and agree who does what.
  • Explain how the measures will reduce the tax risk.
  • Use at least 8 vocabulary items from the glossary accurately and appropriately.
  • Rates, thresholds, and deadlines must match the lesson text.
  • Both partners must speak. Use notes if necessary, but do not simply read a prepared text.
Important. All companies and people in the cases are fictional. Level A gives you key facts and sentence starters. Level B gives you more complex tasks and no starters.
