# Adapting a worksheet to a specialty

Keep the text, glossary, and task logic the same across specialties unless the teacher asks otherwise. Change the professional context so that every specialty sees itself in the sheet. After the change, search the built file for words of the other specialty (`check_docx.py --forbid banker,warehouse,...`). General money words such as bank account and bank card are not context and stay.

## Indices that usually carry the context

| Index | What to change |
|---|---|
| 2 | meta line, specialty name |
| 5, 6, 7 | the situation, the teacher's or professional's quote, the characters' lines |
| 12 | the prediction question |
| text paragraph about work | the professional paragraph of the text, if the text has one |
| 32 | the second cause-and-effect line |
| 100 | the cloze line with a job |
| 115 to 129 | characters' facts (jobs, goals) |
| 139, 140 | opinion and advice tasks |
| 151, 154, 155 | synthesis question and points |
| 158, 159 | Option 1 setting |
| 170 to 174 | Option 4 role play |
| 175 to 178 | Option 5 product (data from the specialty) |
| 196 | last speaking question |

## Banking (38.02.07)

Setting: bank branch, bank consultant, client. Roles (Year 1): teller, loan officer, credit analyst, branch manager, relationship manager, compliance officer. Decision logic: approve, decline, or escalate (credit); flag or clear (AML); recommend or reject (product). Never investment buy, sell, hold. Text focus for Year 2+: banking operations, credit process, Basel III, AML, KYC, customer relationships, retail and corporate products. Core terms for Year 2+: collateral, creditworthiness, loan covenant, default risk, NPL, provisioning, Basel III, AML, KYC, net interest margin, deposit base, regulatory capital, liquidity ratio, LTV, overdraft, mortgage, interest rate risk. Exclude finance terms such as DCF, P/E, EBITDA unless relevant. Year 2+: Option 1 can be a Credit Recommendation Memo, Option 5 a client-facing product, at least one compliance or regulatory task per sheet.

## Finance (38.02.06)

Setting: finance department, analyst, consultant, investor, fintech company, auditor. Year 3 topics link to the demo exam (procurement and price calculation, tax consulting on USN). Role play with an auditor, a tax adviser, or a client.

## Logistics (operational activity in logistics)

Setting: logistics company, warehouse, delivery, transport, supply chain. Roles: warehouse worker, courier, dispatcher, logistics manager, HR manager of a logistics company. Example adaptations that worked: part-time job at a warehouse or as a courier, a logistics manager who plans costs and budgets, Option 4 job interview at a logistics company.

## Economics and Accounting

Setting: accounting office, company finance department, tax office contact. Roles: accountant, young accountant who helps with personal finances, economist, consultant. Example adaptations that worked: a character who writes all income and expenses in a table, saving for an accounting course, Option 4 "My First Budget" with a young accountant, Option 5 "Personal Budget Dashboard" with monthly totals.

## Economics (economist)

Setting: consulting company, analysis of data and reports. An economist works with numbers and with people (colleagues, managers, clients); chess or reading develop analysis, team sports help discuss ideas.
