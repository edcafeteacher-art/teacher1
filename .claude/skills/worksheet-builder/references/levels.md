# Levels and years

The teacher's doctrine has four modes: F1 (Finance Year 1), F2 (Finance Year 2+), B1 (Banking Year 1), B2 (Banking Year 2+). Apply the mode from the specialty and year she gives. Logistics and Economics and Accounting follow the same year rules.

## By year

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical CEFR | A1 to A2 (sometimes B1) | B1 | B2 |
| Glossary | 12 to 14 by default, she often asks for 20 to 30 | all units of her source glossary (35 worked) | about 30, plus professional terms of the case |
| Bold per paragraph | max 6 to 7 | not limited | not limited |

| Critical thinking | binary statement, "I agree / I disagree", guiding prompt for the counterargument | statement from the source, position, 2 reasons, counterargument | statement on a professional dilemma |
| Synthesis | 4 to 5 sentences | 6 to 8 sentences | 8 to 10 sentences |
| Peer assessment | simple language, 3 glossary words | 5 glossary words | 6 glossary words, accuracy of facts |

## By CEFR level

**A1.** Present Simple, short sentences (8 to 12 words), no subordinate clauses beyond because, very common words, definitions in the glossary of A1 words. Characters' quotes are 1 to 2 short sentences.

**A1 to A2 with two text versions in one sheet.** Part 2 holds "Text A1   Easier version" and "Text A2   Standard version" as one list value for index 15 (indices 16 to 19 = `DELETE`). Both versions must have the same number of paragraphs on the same topics in the same order, so every task works for both. Task 1 label says "Match each paragraph of your text (A1 or A2) to its topic."

**A2.** Present Simple and Past Simple, can, should, because, so, as a result, first, second, finally. Replace B1+ words (luxurious, unrealistic expectations, meaningful) with simpler ones or keep them and teach them in the glossary.

**A2 Version 2 (harder sheet).** The teacher's original text, glossary extended (22 to 28), no word box in the cloze and the phrase says so, chain without hints, 2-sentence differences with an example, advice to two characters, counterargument with refutation, synthesis 6 to 8 sentences, more glossary words in Requirements, Language tips with "On the one hand..." and "As a result...".

**A2 middle level ("что-то среднее").** One A2 text, glossary about 25, chain with hints, opinion plus prediction in Part 1, differences with an example, synthesis 5 to 6 sentences.

**B1.** The teacher's B1 text unchanged except dashes and colons. Full task wording of the template.

**B2.** Text of 5 paragraphs of 110 to 150 words, professional register, verified facts, glossary of about 30 with one-line definitions, Part 9 Option 1 can be a role play with a professional, error finding mixes facts and grammar.

## Text adaptation rules

- When you adapt her text, keep its meaning, paragraph order, and the number of paragraphs.
- Tell her exactly which words you replaced.
- When she says "keep the text as is", change only em-dashes (to commas) and colons, and say so.
