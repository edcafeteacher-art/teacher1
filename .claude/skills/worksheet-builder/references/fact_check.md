# Fact check

The teacher requires current data. Any rate, threshold, deadline, law number, price, or statistic in the text, the case, or the tasks must be verified before you write it.

## Procedure

1. List every factual claim the worksheet will make.
2. Search the web for each, in Russian for Russian rules. Prefer primary sources: nalog.gov.ru, consultant.ru, garant.ru, cbr.ru, government sites, the law itself. Use professional media (Клерк, Главбух, 1С ИТС, Моё дело) as secondary confirmation.
3. When two sources disagree, find a third and check whether one of them describes a draft law rather than the adopted one. Use only adopted rules.
4. Do not include future values that may still change. State them to the teacher instead.
5. In the worksheet, prefer general rules ("by the 25th day of the following month", "by 30 April") over dates shifted by weekends.
6. Write the verified facts and their sources as a comment at the top of the content script.
7. In your report to the teacher, give a short Sources list with links and say what you could not confirm.

## Facts verified in October 2026 (re-verify before reuse, rules change)

- VAT standard rate 22 percent from 1 January 2026 (Federal Law No. 425-FZ of 28.11.2025); 10 percent reduced rate kept for some food and children's goods.
- USN in 2026: exempt from VAT if income of the previous year was not more than 20 million roubles; above that, VAT at 5 percent (income up to 272.5 million) or 7 percent (up to 490.5 million) without deductions, or the standard rate with deductions. Thresholds for 2027 and 2028 were under discussion in June 2026; do not state them without checking.
- Personal income tax: progressive scale 13 to 22 percent since 2025.
- Single tax account (ENS) since 2023; single tax payment; notifications of calculated tax when a tax is due before the return.
- USN annual return: companies by 25 March, individual entrepreneurs by 25 April.
- VAT return: quarterly, by the 25th of the following month, electronic only through an EDF operator; tax paid in three equal parts.
- 3-NDFL: by 30 April when obligatory; a return only for a deduction can be filed later.
- Late filing penalty: 5 percent of unpaid tax per month of delay (art. 119 Tax Code).
